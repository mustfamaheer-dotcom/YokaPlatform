

## 🏢 Quadfare Platform — Complete Business Feature Map

---

### 👤 User Roles & Access Control
The platform has **5 distinct user roles**, each with its own dedicated interface:

| Role | Arabic Title | Dedicated App |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `super_admin` | مدير عام النظام | Full Admin Dashboard |
| `admin` | مدير إداري | Full Admin Dashboard |
| `warehouse_manager` | مدير المخازن الرئيسي | Warehouse Manager App (with granular permissions) |
| `supervisor` | مشرف فرع | Supervisor Dashboard (branch-scoped) |
| `salesperson` | بائع / كاشير | POS Cashier App |

---

### 🖥️ 1. نقطة البيع (POS – Cashier Terminal)
- **Barcode scanning** for instant product lookup
- **Sale mode** and **Return/Refund mode** switchable from the same terminal
- Loyalty points **lookup by phone**, points earning and redemption at checkout
- Receipt printing (browser print)
- **Shift open/close** with cash count and handover
- **Expense entry** directly from cashier screen (linked to expense categories)
- **Stock alerts badge** — cashier sees low-stock warnings on their nav
- **Supervisor unlock** — supervisor can unlock advanced functions without logging out the cashier
- **Exit backup modal** — safe data backup before logging out
- Responsive design (tablet and desktop)

---

### 🛒 2. المتجر الإلكتروني (E-Commerce Management)
- **Orders management** — receive, process, track, ship online orders
- **Pending orders live counter** — auto-refreshes every 20 seconds
- **E-commerce warehouse inventory** — dedicated stock pool for online orders, separate from retail
- **E-commerce analytics** — visitor traffic analysis, digital sales, average basket value
- Dedicated **Ecom Warehouse App** for staff who only handle online order fulfillment

---

### 📦 3. إدارة المخزون (Inventory Management)
- **Product catalog** — full product database with attributes
- **Groups & Items tree** — category/subcategory classification, sizes, colors, product variants
- **Physical stock audit** — field inventory counting, real-time variance detection, deficit/surplus handling
- **Adjustment vouchers** — approve and post stock correction entries
- **Inter-branch transfers** — transfer stock between branches and warehouses with transfer vouchers
- **Stock alerts** — low-stock threshold alerts per branch
- **Multi-branch stock levels** — each branch has its own isolated stock balance

---

### 🏗️ 4. مدير المخازن الرئيسي (Warehouse Manager App)
A separate role-based app with **granular permission toggles** per feature:
- Product & category management
- Physical stock audit
- Inter-branch transfers
- Purchase invoices
- Supplier accounts
- Payroll & expense categories
- Branch & user management
- Each permission independently toggleable by the super_admin

---

### 🧾 5. المشتريات والتوريد (Purchasing & Supply Chain)
- **Purchase invoices** — create, review, and record supplier invoices with unit cost
- **Supplier payments** — partial and full payment tracking, outstanding balances
- **Supplier ledger** — detailed account statement per supplier (debits, credits, balance)
- **Supplier directory** — full supplier contact and account registry

---

### 💰 6. المالية والخزائن (Finance & Treasury)
- **Central treasury** — track cash flow across all branches centrally
- **Bank transfers** — record bank-to-branch and branch-to-bank transfers
- **Branch cash reset (تصفير الخزائن)** — clear branch cash after handover
- **Payroll management** — salesperson salary and commission settlement
- **Advances & deductions** — advance salary payments and paycheck deductions
- **Expense categories** — custom expense types per branch (e.g., rent, cleaning, delivery)
- **Branch daily expense tracking** — cashier-entered expenses linked to categories

---

### 📊 7. التقارير والتحليلات (Reports & Analytics)
- **Central Sales Analytics Dashboard** — charts, KPIs, revenue trends
- **Branch performance comparison** — side-by-side branch revenue and sales counts
- **Top selling products** — best sellers by branch and system-wide
- **Returns analysis** — return volumes and reasons
- **Salesperson performance** — revenue and transaction count per staff member
- **Branches daily consolidated ledger** — daily cash, sales, and expense statement per branch
- **Admin journals** — audit log of admin-level operations and stock movements
- **Shift monitoring** — live view of which branches are open, cashier clock-in/out history
- **Ecom analytics** — online store visitor stats and conversion data

---

### 👥 8. سجل العملاء ونقاط الولاء (Customers & Loyalty CRM)
- **Customer registry** — central directory of all registered customers
- **Loyalty points system** — earn points per purchase, redeem at POS
- **Lifetime points tracking** — total earned vs. currently available
- **Manual points adjustment** — admin can credit/debit points with reason log
- **Branch filter** — filter customers by retail branch
- **Quick customer registration** — from POS checkout or admin panel
- **Customer ledger drawer** — full purchase history and points transaction log per customer
- **Excel/CSV export** — export full customer list with UTF-8 BOM for Arabic
- **Loyalty settings configuration** — point value, earn rate, redemption rules

---

### ⚙️ 9. إدارة النظام (System Administration)
- **Branch & warehouse management** — create/edit retail branches and warehouses, set branch type
- **User accounts & roles** — create users, assign roles and branch access
- **System backup** — export data backup before logout (ExitBackupModal)
- **Store settings** — configure store-wide settings (invoice design, loyalty rules, etc.)
- **Invoice settings** — customize receipt/invoice layout
- **Supervisor unlock** — temporary role elevation without full login switch
- **Visitor analytics** — track who accessed what and when

---

### 📱 10. Cross-Cutting Technical Features
- **RTL Arabic-first UI** — entire interface is Arabic right-to-left
- **Role-based routing** — users automatically land in their correct app upon login
- **Multi-branch architecture** — every transaction, stock level, and report is branch-scoped
- **JWT authentication** — stateless, secure session management
- **Responsive design** — works on desktop, tablet, and mobile
- **Activity audit log** — every create/update/delete action is logged with user ID and timestamp

---

### Summary Count
> **9 functional modules · 5 user roles · 4 distinct interfaces (Admin, POS, Warehouse, Ecom) · 40+ individual feature screens**
